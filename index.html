How to play

  1. 👆Drag anywhere below the middle, or on the rail under the court, to move your paddle.
  2. ↝Flick sideways as the ball lands to curve it. The computer cannot read a curve.
  3. ✹Supers: returns fill your meter. When it glows, flick as you hit to fire it.
  4. ◐Hit off-center to angle your return. Every hit speeds the ball up.
  5. ✦Grab power-ups with the ball: Wide, Shrink, Turbo, Ghost and Multiball. Whoever hit last gets it.
  6. ✈Your paddle is a starship. It fires by itself: steer out of the way of rocks and fire, and under what you want shot down.
  7. ➤Your paddle is a racing craft. It flies by itself: thread rings and gates, jump ramps, drift round bends, slipstream rivals.
  8. ◎Fight for your place. Guns fire at rivals ahead by themselves. The side panels add nitro, gun overdrive, rockets, a shield and your craft's warp.
  9. ✦Steer with the buttons under the track, a floating stick or classic drag: tap STEER to switch. Keys: N G R F E for the panels.
  10. ▦Break every wall. Catch what falls with your paddle. A super goes through bricks, or takes a whole row.
